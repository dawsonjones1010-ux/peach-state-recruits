FAQ

Questions, answered

What do we do?

Peach State Recruits helps high school and travel ball baseball players get seen. For $25, we make you a custom cover graphic, write a scouting-style breakdown of your game, and post it as a collab on @peachstaterecruits so players and coaches see your clips. Every purchase also comes with your own player profile page here, and our free Find My Level tool shows you how your numbers stack up for college. To get started, just send us a DM on Instagram.

How do I order and pay?

For now, every order goes through Instagram. Message @peachstaterecruits with the package you want and we'll reply with how to pay. Online checkout isn't available yet.

What do I need to send?

Your best clips, one good photo for the cover, and your position, grad year, school or team and any stats you want shown. You can send it all in the Instagram DM.

How long does it take?

Most features are finished within a few days of receiving everything. Elite orders are handled first. We'll give you a delivery date once your clips are in.

Who do you feature?

Baseball players: high school and travel ball, every position, pitchers and position players. If you have game or practice clips, we can build your feature.

Can parents order for their player?

Yes, and most do. For players under 18, a parent or guardian approves what goes on a public page.

What's on a player's profile page?

The feature cover, film, measurables, school or team, hometown and a short coach summary. The contact button goes to an email the family approves. Until a family adds one, it goes to Peach State on Instagram and we pass messages along. We never show a home address or phone number.

Do you guarantee offers or scholarships?

No, and nobody honest can. We make you look professional and make your game easy for coaches to see. Your play and your outreach do the rest.

How long does my post stay up?

Your Instagram feature stays up as long as you want it to.
