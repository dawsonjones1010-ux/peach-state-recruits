FAQ

Questions, answered

Which sports do you cover?

All of them, including baseball, football, soccer, basketball, softball, volleyball, lacrosse and track. If you have clips, we can feature you.

What do I need to send?

Your best clips, one good photo for the cover, and your position, grad year, school or team and any stats you want shown. After checkout you'll get a short form to upload everything.

How long does it take?

Most features are finished within a few days of receiving everything. Elite orders are handled first. You'll get a delivery date once your clips are in.

Can parents order for their athlete?

Yes, and most do. For athletes under 18, a parent or guardian approves what goes on a public profile.

What's shown on a profile page?

Your feature, video, stats, measurables, school or team, city and state, and a contact button to the email you approve. We never show a home address or phone number.

Do you guarantee offers or scholarships?

No, and nobody honest can. We make you look professional and make your game easy for coaches to see. Your play and your outreach do the rest.

How long does my post stay up?

As long as you want. Profile pages stay live for the year you've paid for and can be renewed.

Can I pay another way?

Card checkout is fastest. If you need another option, message us on Instagram.
