Terms of Use

The basics for using our site and free recruiting tools.

Last updated November 21, 2026

These terms cover your use of the Peach State Recruits website and its free tools (Find My Level, College Finder, Camp Finder, Email Coach and My Recruiting). By using the site, you agree to them. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian should review them with you. Also see our Privacy Policy.

What we are

Peach State Recruits is a baseball media brand. We create player content and recruiting tools. We are not a recruiting agency, and we don't guarantee college interest, offers, roster spots or scholarships.

Our tools are guides, not promises

  • Find My Level compares the metrics you enter with published benchmarks. It's an estimate, not an evaluation by a coach, and it doesn't predict where you'll play.
  • College Finder and Camp Finder come from public listings and official pages. Programs, rosters, dates, prices and rules change. Always confirm on the school's or event's official page before you act or pay.
  • Recruiting rules and dates are summaries with links to their sources. You and your family are responsible for following NCAA, NAIA and NJCAA rules. Check with the official source or a school's compliance office.
  • Email Coach and My Recruiting help you write and track messages. You choose what to send and are responsible for what you send.

Your account

You must be 13 or older to create an account. Keep access to your email secure, since that's how you sign in. Use your own real information and don't sign in as someone else.

What you share with us

You keep ownership of your photos, videos, stats and other content. When you buy a feature or send us content, you give us permission to use it to make your post, video and player profile and to show them on our Instagram accounts and website. You can ask us to take your profile down at any time. Only send content you have the right to share.

Orders

Prices and what each package includes are listed under Packages. Orders are confirmed through Instagram messages. If something isn't right with your order, message us and we'll work with you to fix it.

Please don't

  • Post false information about yourself or anyone else, or use someone else's name, photos or film without permission.
  • Use the tools to spam coaches or anyone else.
  • Copy our directory or content in bulk, use bots on the site, or try to get into accounts or data that aren't yours.

Not affiliated

We aren't affiliated with or endorsed by the NCAA, NAIA, NJCAA, any college, or any camp or registration service. School and camp names belong to their owners and are used only to identify them.

No warranty, limited liability

The site and tools are provided "as is". We work to keep information accurate but can't guarantee it's complete, current or error-free. To the extent the law allows, we're not responsible for decisions you make based on the site, such as camp fees, travel or which schools you contact.

Changes

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version. Questions: peachstaterecruitshq@gmail.com.
