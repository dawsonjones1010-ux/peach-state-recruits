# Research and evidence policy

Checked November 20, 2026. Publisher names and URLs are attribution, not sponsorship, endorsement or a guarantee. Advice was paraphrased and templates were newly written. The distinction between an official rule, a publisher guideline and an observed sample mean remains visible.

| Source | What it supports in this update | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [NCAA Division I Manual](https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008) | Baseball-specific communication versus visit/off-campus contact rules. General D1 baseball communication starts August 1 before junior year; visits/contact have their separate September 1 junior-year restriction. | Traditional U.S. high-school calendar; not a current-college transfer or signed-aid ruling. Recruiting calendar periods and compliance review still matter. |
| [NCAA Division II Manual](https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90010) | D2 communication can occur at any time under 13.1.3.1; first high-school off-campus contact/official visits have separate timing. | Do not treat communication as permission for a visit. Signed aid and enrollment can impose other restrictions. |
| [NCAA recruiting education](https://www.ncaa.org/eligibility-center/recruiting/) | Recruiting periods, athlete initiative, academics and school evaluation context. | Does not publish universal athletic minimums or an offer-probability model. |
| [NCSA baseball recruiting guidelines](https://www.ncsasports.org/baseball/recruiting-guidelines) | Independent position-tool cross-check: source-published speed, arm/pop and game-fastball guidance. | All D1 pooled; D3/NAIA pooled. Not NCAA minimums. Best pop time does not prove consistency; peak fastball does not replace typical game velocity. Unknown targets stay unknown. |
| [NCSA email education](https://www.ncsasports.org/recruiting/contacting-college-coaches/email) | Identifying subjects, real player details, personalization, video and an action question. Its 50–75 character subject guidance informs a length warning. | No universal “best send time” or predicted reply rate. The 220-word trimming warning is our editorial choice, not a tested cutoff. |
| [Keep Playing Baseball: introductory emails](https://old.keepplayingbaseball.org/contacting-college-coaches-how-to-write-an-email/) | Player-led introductions, a concise school reason, accessible video, schedules, questionnaires and coach references. | Older article: used for message structure only. Its old communication dates are not copied; current NCAA sources control timing. |
| [ABCA Inside Pitch coach interviews](https://www.abca.org/magazine/magazine/2014-4-Fall/Inside_Interview_How_to_Get_Recruited.aspx) | Evaluation needs video, trusted coach context and overall fit, beyond isolated metrics. | 2014 interviews are general baseball advice, not current recruiting legislation or numeric thresholds. |
| [MLB exit velocity](https://www.mlb.com/glossary/statcast/exit-velocity) | Batted-ball speed definition. | Not a college standard. |
| [MLB pop time](https://www.mlb.com/glossary/statcast/pop-time) | Catcher receive-to-fielder-receive timing. | Best result, accuracy and repeatability need separate context. |

The original export supplied NextCommit category targets, BaseballPath commit averages, Flatground and Slammers samples, Prep Baseball cohorts, Pitching Coach U and research-sample norms. Those original datasets were retained with their provenance and limitations; this update did not invent replacements or claim every original publication was newly revalidated. Seven-category headline status still uses the disclosed original NextCommit product rule; it is not an NCAA evaluation. The independent NCSA display is a separate cross-check, not a blended score or independent proof of the same seven tiers.

NCSA's numeric cross-check keeps its actual published grouping and wording where it changes the meaning: center-field speed described as “below” is checked strictly below the boundary; pitcher checks use entered game velocity instead of a peak; a published velocity band is not treated as a ceiling. The source does not provide an exact usable numeric row for every position/tool/level in our checked records, and those gaps remain explicit.

What this product intentionally does not infer: a player's eventual college, scholarship value, academic admission, roster opening, percentage chance of an offer, verified status from a link, statistically significant improvement from a tiny difference, future growth from age/height, or program interest from an email open. None of those has been validated by this source package.

Product interpretation choices are labeled: six months is a freshness reminder, not a physiological expiry; 0.10 s / 1 mph trigger small-gap cautions, not statistical error estimates; next actions are suggestions, not a prescribed training plan; hypothetical inputs are never mixed with actual results; draft checks measure completeness, not recruiting ability.
