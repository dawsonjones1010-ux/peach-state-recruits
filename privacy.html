Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how to delete it. In short: we don't sell your information, your recruiting account is private to you, and visitor counts use no cookies.

Last updated November 20, 2026

Peach State Recruits ("we", "us") makes player features, highlight videos, player profiles and free recruiting tools for high school baseball players. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: peachstaterecruitshq@gmail.com.

Who our tools are for

Our tools are for high school and junior college players, their parents and their coaches. You must be 13 or older to create an account or send us information. If you're under 18, use our tools with your parent or guardian's knowledge. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

What we collect and why

Find My Level

The metrics you enter stay on your device (in your browser's storage) so you can see your history. They're sent to us only if you tap Send to ask for a free look. Then we receive your Instagram handle, class, positions, bats/throws, the metrics you entered, how they were measured and your result, so we can reply to you on Instagram.

My Recruiting, College Finder and Camp Finder accounts

To sign in, you give us your email address and we email you a sign-in link. Anything you save is stored in your private account: your recruiting profile (name, class, school, team, position, size, academics, metrics, contact details and links), saved schools and notes, coach names and emails you enter, email drafts, the messages you log, your plan and saved camps. Only you can see this information. As the site owner, we see only totals (for example, how many schools have been saved), not your details.

Featured player profiles

When you buy a feature, we collect the information you or your parent send us (name, school, team, position, class, height, weight, stats, academics, links, photos and video) to make your Instagram post and your public player profile. Your profile is public on purpose so coaches can find it. We only publish what you've provided, and you can ask us to change or remove it at any time. If we give you a player login, you can edit your own profile.

Orders

Orders happen through Instagram messages. We keep a record of the order: buyer and player name, how to contact you, the package, the price and whether it's paid and delivered.

Visitor counts

We count page visits without cookies. For each page view we store only the page, the name of the website that sent you (for example instagram.com), any campaign tag in the link (like utm_source) and whether you're on a phone, tablet or computer. We don't store your IP address or any identifier, and we can't tell who you are. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we don't count your visit. Player profile pages also count views the same anonymous way.

Recruiting Roadmap worksheets

Your planning checkmarks, camp answers and college-cost entries stay in the current page unless you choose “Keep my worksheet on this device.” That option stores them in this browser, separate from My Recruiting. Anyone using the same browser can access them. Use “Clear worksheet” or clear this site’s browser data to remove the saved copy. Copying or printing creates a copy you control.

Your browser's storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember things on your device, like your Find My Level history, email drafts and whether you've already been counted this visit. We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, share it for advertising or send it to recruiting services. We don't email coaches for you: you decide what to send and send it yourself.

Services we use

These companies host or deliver parts of the site and handle data only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: our database and email sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website. Like most web hosts, it may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver fonts and code to your browser, which means they receive your IP address.
  • Instagram (Meta): orders and messages, and Instagram posts you choose to play on a profile.

How long we keep it

We keep account data until you delete it or ask us to. Free-look requests are kept while we review them and for our records. Visitor counts contain no personal information.

Your choices

  • Delete your recruiting data yourself: in My Recruiting, open Profile and tap Delete my recruiting data.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete anything else, including your sign-in account, a free-look request or a featured profile: email peachstaterecruitshq@gmail.com. We'll answer within 30 days. A parent or guardian can make these requests for a player under 18.
  • Clear what's stored on your device by clearing your browser's site data.

Security

Your account data is protected by row-level security, so each account can only read its own records. No system is perfect, so don't save anything in your notes you wouldn't want exposed, like passwords or ID numbers.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will be announced on the site.
